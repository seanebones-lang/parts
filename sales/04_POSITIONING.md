## Market narrative

Dealership fixed ops is squeezed on labor and CSI. “AI” vendors flood inboxes with chat UIs. Most cannot show a ranked parts list with stock posture, a graded email desk, and fail-closed confidence.

**Parts** owns the narrative: **retrieval + policy + multi-store ops + email desk + audited automation**, not chat theater.

---

## Category name we own

**Multi-Location Parts Intelligence (MLPI)** / dealership parts OS

Avoid being boxed as:
- “Just another chatbot”
- “Full DMS replacement next month”
- “Fully autonomous parts manager”

---

## Message hierarchy

1. **Find the part across rooftops in plain English**  
2. **Know when a human must take it** (traffic-light + email G/Y/R)  
3. **Run the desk and the order path** (DMS + notify + `/results`)  
4. **Integrate when ready** (OEM feed, Stripe, EasyPost, SMTP — fail closed)  
5. **Measure the pilot** (hit-rate, latency, overrides)
