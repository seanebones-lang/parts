## The problem

Parts counters and advisors still burn minutes on every “Do you have…?”  
Across multiple rooftops, the answer is often a phone tree, a hope, or a wrong promise — and inbound email piles up without a graded handoff.

## The product

**Parts** turns plain English into ranked multi-store availability — with a **green / yellow / red** signal — plus a production **email desk**, offline **DMS ops**, **automation results**, and **customer notify** drafts when you’re ready to connect mail.

> “Brake pads for a 2019 Honda Civic” → SKUs, locations, stock posture, confidence.
