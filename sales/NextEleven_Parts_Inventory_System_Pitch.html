Customers don’t speak SKU. They speak cars, symptoms, and urgency. Your team answers the same questions by phone, email, and counter — while stock lives across rooftops, DMS screens, and tribal memory.

“Do you have brake pads for a 2019 Civic?” should not require three people and twenty minutes.

One product surface for the work parts people already do — search, email, stock, orders, transfers, feeds, and (when you’re ready) payments and shipping.

AI parts search that dealers can defend

Hybrid retrieval combines dense semantic matching with BM25 keyword search (RRF fusion), optional parent expansion across locations for the same base SKU, and a traffic-light policy driven by similarity × stock — not vibes.

Counter language → ranked availability

query · no unsupervised “hallucinated SKU” as truth

“Brake pads for 2019 Honda Civic” → ranked hits with location, stock posture, price signals, and green/yellow/red so the advisor knows whether to quote, confirm, or escalate. LLM phrasing is optional and off by default for pure retrieval demos.

Why hybrid matters for parts

Parts language mixes OEM numbers, aftermarket slang, vehicle fitment, and typos. Pure keyword misses meaning; pure vector misses exact SKU discipline. Fusion keeps both.

Multi-location without the phone tree

Same catalog model across stores. Expand siblings by base SKU so “we’re out here” can become “we have two at Logan” in one response path.

Transfer control · threshold behavior (default qty ≥ 10 → manager)

Path A — small transfer

Counter submits qty below threshold → status completed immediately. Total SKU quantity across stores unchanged.

Path B — large transfer

Qty at/above PARRTS_TRANSFER_APPROVAL_QTY → pending_approval until manager. No stock move until approved.

Configurable threshold. Counter cannot self-approve large moves. Cancel leaves stock untouched.

OEM & distributor feeds — adapters, not scraping

Catalog truth comes from your export or a contracted HTTP feed — never unauthorized portal scraping, never invented partner APIs.

file

JSON/CSV drop from distributor or DMS export → sync → reindex.

http

OEM_FEED_URL + optional token. Fail closed if unset.

nightly beat

Celery oem.scheduled_sync (default 06:00 UTC). Skips clean without URL.

Ops visibility: every sync writes oem_sync_runs — last run on DMS status, full list via API/CLI. You can prove what ran and when.
